### Procedure

<h2 style="color: rgb(237,229,225); font-family: Arial;">Procedure</h2>

<ol style="font-size:17px; font-family:Arial; color:#000000; line-height:1.6;">

  <li>Begin by switching on the system and loading the swaging simulation or setting up the physical machine for operation.</li>

  <li>Carefully clean the cylindrical workpiece or tube to remove any dust, oil, or surface contaminants before placing it inside the die.</li>

  <li>If the experiment requires forming an internal profile, insert the mandrel into the hollow workpiece and ensure proper fit and alignment.</li>

  <li>Place the workpiece accurately in the swaging die so that it is properly centered and aligned with the rotary hammering mechanism.</li>

  <li>Check the positioning of the split dies and ensure that they are free from obstruction and correctly mounted in the spindle.</li>

  <li>Switch on the swaging machine to allow the dies to rotate and open–close rapidly under centrifugal force.</li>

  <li>Observe how the repeated radial hammering action compresses the workpiece and gradually reduces its diameter or forms the desired taper/profile.</li>

  <li>Monitor the deformation process continuously to ensure uniform metal flow and prevent misalignment or bending of the workpiece.</li>

  <li>Continue the swaging operation until the desired final dimensions or internal/external shape is achieved.</li>

  <li>After completing the operation, switch off the machine and allow the dies to come to a complete stop before removing the workpiece.</li>

  <li>Carefully remove the workpiece from the die and mandrel (if used) and place it on a safe surface for cooling or inspection.</li>

  <li>Measure the final dimensions—outer diameter, inner shape (if mandrel was used), and length—to compare them with the expected values.</li>

  <li>Examine the surface finish and check for defects such as uneven taper, cracks, or folds that may have occurred during deformation.</li>

  <li>Record all observations and finalize the experiment report with deformation characteristics and results.</li>

</ol>
