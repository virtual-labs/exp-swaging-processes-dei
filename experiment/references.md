### References

1. Kalpakjian, S., & Schmid, S. R. (2014). *Manufacturing Processes for Engineering Materials*. Pearson Education.  
   — Provides fundamental explanation of metal forming processes including swaging and radial forging.

2. Groover, M. P. (2020). *Fundamentals of Modern Manufacturing: Materials, Processes, and Systems*. Wiley.  
   — Covers tube swaging, cold working, and applications in manufacturing industries.

3. ASM International. (2005). *ASM Handbook, Volume 14: Forming and Forging*. ASM International.  
   — Detailed technical reference on rotary swaging, radial forging machines, and tooling.

4. Lange, K. (1985). *Handbook of Metal Forming*. McGraw-Hill.  
   — Classic reference describing swaging mechanisms, mandrels, and deformation behavior.

5. Schuler Group. (2012). *Metal Forming Handbook*. Springer.  
   — Industrial-focused explanation of rotary swaging machines, applications, and process advantages.
