

<ol style="font-size:17px; font-family:Arial; color:#000000; line-height:1.6;">

  <li>Start the swaging simulation or switch on the swaging machine.</li>

  <li>Clean and prepare the cylindrical workpiece or tube before the operation.</li>

  <li>Insert the workpiece carefully into the swaging die.</li>

  <li>If required, place the mandrel inside the hollow workpiece for internal shaping.</li>

  <li>Ensure proper alignment of the workpiece with the rotary swaging dies.</li>

  <li>Set the required speed and operating parameters of the swaging machine.</li>

  <li>Allow the dies to rotate and apply radial compressive forces on the workpiece.</li>

  <li>Observe the gradual reduction in diameter or change in profile of the workpiece.</li>

  <li>Stop the machine once the desired shape and dimensions are achieved.</li>

  <li>Remove the swaged component and inspect it for accuracy and surface finish.</li>

</ol>
